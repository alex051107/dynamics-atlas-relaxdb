# Bacillus subtilis SigA (σA) domain 1.1

RelaxDB entry `27011` · set RelaxDB-133 · Bacillus subtilis SigA (σA) domain 1.1, author residues 1–71 plus LE-His6

## Answer

**Yes: a minor state is measured in the free protein.**

**Strict two-peak reading:** does not (computed). The authors call it 'slow exchange' in the µs-ms sense, but kex ~3,500 (printed as Hz) against 15N Δω 2-6 ppm gives kex/Δω about 1.0-4.6 at 600-950 MHz, i.e. intermediate to fast, and no minor-state peaks are shown (computed by Claude 2026-10-20 after the night review; replaces 'qualifies by wording only').

Model-free fits at three fields need exchange terms at many residues in helix I and the regions packed against it. 15N CPMG at 600, 850 and 950 MHz fits a two-state process with a fairly uniform rate and a representative 1.5% minor state whose 15N shifts differ by 2–6 ppm. The per-residue fits are figure-only, and the minor-state structure is unknown.

## Caveats

No structure is matched: 5MWW is the dominant free fold, and the authors' suggestion that the minor state resembles the T. maritima fold is speculative (SIGA-r3, SIGA-r6). The 4% state of the 2023 abstract cannot be assumed to be the 1.5% CPMG state. In the BMRB 27011 deposition the R2 error column exceeds the value in most rows, so the columns may be swapped; this deposition screen does not set the call. kex is kept as printed ('~3,500 Hz').
