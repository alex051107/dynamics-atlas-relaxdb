# Third IgG-binding domain of streptococcal protein G (GB3)

RelaxDB entry `5839` · set RelaxDB-133 · Third IgG-binding domain of streptococcal protein G (GB3), 56 residues, entry sequence with Q2

## Answer

**Possible: exchange is detected in the free protein, but no population is measured.**

**Strict two-peak reading:** cannot tell. No regime is stated for the room-temperature wild-type processes and no second peak set is reported; the only stated regime is fast exchange, in perdeuterated GB3 at 262–275 K.

Room-temperature amide 15N relaxation of protonated wild-type GB3 is nearly silent. V39 is the one amide that needs an exchange term at all five fields and in every tumbling model; the helix Rex is an anisotropic-tumbling artefact, and relaxation-compensated CPMG finds no dispersion up to about 8 ms. Multiple-quantum relaxation detects slow correlated shift fluctuations (Cα–Cβ at 17 residues, C'–N over most of the backbone) with rate ranges but no population or minor state. Microsecond exchange with measured lifetimes appears only in perdeuterated GB3 below room temperature.

## Other conditions (not the reference state)

- Perdeuterated GB3, 262–275 K, pH 6.5, 50 mM sodium phosphate, 100 mM NaCl: possible. First-turn amide 1H R1rho, G9–K13 share τex 35.9 ± 1.3 µs (262 K) to 9.1 ± 0.4 µs (275 K), fast exchange, population not separable (GB3-J47, GB3-5839_20261017120337-r5 to GB3-5839_20261017120337-r8); individual fits 7.1–16 µs at K10, L12, K13 at 275 K (GB3-J51, GB3-5839_20261017120337-r18). Backbone 15N profiles are flat (GB3-J48).
- Perdeuterated GB3 with 13CHD2 ILV methyls, 275 K: possible. Seven methyl carbons exchange with τex 11–58 µs (GB3 average 27 µs), the methyl proton only at V54 γ2 (13 ± 0.88 µs); population not separable, and the rotamer fractions are model populations (GB3-J50, GB3-5839_20261017120337-r16, GB3-5839_20261017120337-r17, GB3-5839_20261017120337-r19).
- Extrapolation to 310 K, not a measurement: first-turn τex 371 ± 115 ns (Arrhenius) or 409 ± 126 ns (Eyring) (GB3-J19, GB3-5839_20261017120337-r9, GB3-r20).
- T11K/K19E/V42E mutant, deuterated amides, 283 versus 298 K: possible. Extra R1rho at N8, T16, T17, N37 and T53 that shrinks on warming; no dispersion, rate or population (GB3-J17, GB3-r9).
- E27Q mutant, 298 K, amide H/D exchange: β2 amides exchange within the 0.5 h dead time and β1 partners over hours; solvent-exchange constants, not opening rates (GB3-J41, GB3-r46, GB3-r47).

## Caveats

The BMRB 5839 deposition screen flags V39 by R2/R1, and its helix flags (K28, K31, Q32, D36) fit the anisotropic tumbling the 2003 paper identified; the screen does not set the call. The Cα–Cβ rate uses perdeuterated GB3 as an approximate CSA baseline, so baseline and rate are coupled. The V39 Rex depends on the tumbling model, and the pH 6.3 four-field study does not say whether V39 is an exception. Crystal alternate side-chain conformers (1IGD/2IGD) and RDC rotamer populations describe side-chain heterogeneity, not a minor backbone state, so no structure is matched (GB3-J28, GB3-J31). the NMR collaborator is asked whether the perdeuterated cold-sample exchange should count toward the WT apo answer.

## Open questions

- Backbone ps-ns amplitude heterogeneity: the five-field Fushman analysis finds large site-to-site 15N CSA variation that moves F30 S2 from 0.93 to 0.81 (r2), while the RCSA-calibrated Bax analysis finds near-uniform S2 0.888 ± 0.017 versus 0.892 ± 0.025 with either CSA treatment (r10); the row notes the discrepancy is not settled by choosing one study.
- V39 exchange: Rex at all five fields at 24 °C, pH 5.8 (r3) and in every tumbling model at two fields (0.28-0.78 s-1, 17-r3) versus backbone 15N broadening of about 0.15 s-1 or less at 25 °C, pH 6.3 (r26); r26 does not say whether V39 is an exception, and the samples differ in pH.
- The protonated Calpha-Cbeta kex of 100-160 s-1 (-r4) uses the perdeuterated sample as an approximate CSA baseline (-r5), so if deuteration itself suppresses the slow motion the baseline and the rate are coupled.
- F52 side chain: single rotamer in the RDC analysis (r15) versus slight averaging in some scalar couplings that the compilation excludes (r38).
- Probe contrast, not a conflict: at 275 K the first turn disperses on amide 1H (J47, J51) but not on 15N (J48), and seven ILV carbons disperse while only V54 does on the methyl proton (J50); the authors attribute this to shift contrast at each nucleus.
- Is the RelaxDB 5839 R1/R2/NOE set the Hall and Fushman 2003 deposition? The deposited conditions (297 K, pH 5.8, 600 MHz, E15, T25, E27 and N35 absent) match that paper, but no source names the accession.
- Should the WT apo us-ms answer count the multiple-quantum kex bounds (protonated, 25 °C) and the first-turn and methyl microsecond exchange measured only in perdeuterated GB3 at 262-275 K, or only room-temperature amide 15N data, which show exchange at V39 alone?
