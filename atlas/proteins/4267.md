## Other conditions (not the reference state)

- Candesartan-bound C87S siderocalin, 25 °C, pH 6.0 phosphate/DMSO: possible. More than one bound peak set; an induced-fit closing step of 7.9 s-1 and a parallel two-site model fit equally well, so a bound-state conformational change is not established (R4267-J14).
- Bound to the SLC22A17 receptor N-terminal domain, 25 °C, pH 7.4: severe NGAL line broadening and receptor-observed binding exchange (kex 1200-1300 s-1), binding kinetics (R4267-J12, J13).
- Enterobactin-bound (aferric and ferric), 37 °C PBS: HDX protection of calyx peptides and reduced Lys/Arg labelling; not an exchange measurement (R4267-J5 to J7).
- Apo and ligand-bound crystals: W79/R81 rotamer alternatives with no concerted backbone change across 73 molecules (R4267-J8, J9).

## Caveats

The entry-associated relaxation paper (doi:10.1006/jmbi.1999.2755) is abstract only, so the call is unknown by lack of readable data, not by a negative result. No deposition readback file was present. Recombinant NGAL can carry copurified siderophores, so 'apo' needs a ligand-removal check. Ligand-bound and receptor-bound states are kept out of the call. Second read raised no flags for this case.

## Open questions

- Is BMRB 4267 the 15N relaxation set of Coles et al. 1999 (doi:10.1006/jmbi.1999.2755, apo 1NGL)? If so, its full text or the deposited R1/R2/NOE values are needed before any window verdict for the entry.
- Recombinant NGAL often carries copurified bacterial siderophores (the receptor study refolded NGAL from guanidinium to remove them; the mouse candesartan sample may contain aferric enterobactin). Should 'apo' for this entry require such a ligand-removal step?
