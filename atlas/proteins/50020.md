## Answer

**Unknown: not measured, or the key paper is not readable in full.**

**Strict two-peak reading:** cannot tell. Neither source states an exchange regime for the free domain or shows two peak sets. The only regime word in either paper, fast exchange on the chemical-shift timescale, describes binding to mFNIII-9 (KD 0.8 +/- 0.3 mM by SPR), which is a binding process (R50020-J8).

No measurement of conformational exchange exists for free vWFA2. The relaxation study reports ps-ns loop flexibility at N1133, N1219, around A1205 and T1208 (R50020-J1) and the RDC outliers mark local solution-crystal differences without a timescale (R50020-J2, J3); no dispersion, R1rho, CEST or exchange-broadening experiment was done (R50020-J4). The 2011 paper, read in full on 2026-10-20, adds no relaxation data: its flexibility statements are a disagreement between scalar couplings and the TALOS+ prediction over residues 78-87 (entry 83-92), which the authors say 'may be in a flexible loop' (R50020-J6), 16 amides still protected after three days in D2O (R50020-J7), unassigned affinity-tag residues (R50020-J9), and fast-exchange binding to mFNIII-9 (R50020-J8). The single-field Rex rule does not apply, because no Rex is reported in either paper.

## Caveats

Call stays unknown after the second RelaxDB-linked source (doi:10.1016/j.febslet.2011.04.071) was read in full on 2026-10-20: that paper measures no relaxation and reports no exchange contribution, so the single-field Rex rule has nothing to act on, and its flexibility statements are a secondary-structure disagreement and a two-point hydrogen-exchange protection map. Its construct carries an unstated residual intein tag and the entry's GRAM prefix is not part of its numbered sequence, so those rows are kept as another construct; the entry = author + 5 mapping was fixed from the sequence printed on the Fig. 3a/3b axis (ledger/adjudication_pdf_batch2.json). The hydrogen-exchange sample is in 100% D2O at nominal pH 7.4 without a pD correction. The deposition flags seven residues by R2/R1 (author G1085, H1143, V1158, I1178, L1186, G1195, G1222) that neither paper discusses; a lead only. The poorly defined alpha5-beta6 loop in 6S4C is static crystal evidence, not a second state.
