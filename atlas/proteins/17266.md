# Cupriavidus metallidurans CH34 SilB C-terminal domain (SilB 440–521)

RelaxDB entry `17266` · set RelaxDB-133 · Cupriavidus metallidurans CH34 SilB C-terminal domain (SilB 440–521), isolated

## Answer

**Possible: exchange is detected in the free protein, but no population is measured.**

**Strict two-peak reading:** cannot tell. The source states no exchange regime, prints no kex, population or Delta-omega and shows no second peak set for the apo domain; it speaks of slow motions of the metal-binding loop and states that the apo protein does not simply exchange with a conformation close to the metal-bound state (SILB-J2, J4; E-r1, E-r2). Without kex and Delta-omega the E-033 computation cannot be made. The one peak that does disappear, the Met65 methyl in the Ag+-bound sample, is a bound-state observation presumed to be line broadening (SILB-J7).

Reference state: in the RelaxDB source sample (apo domain, 25 °C, pH 6.0, 600 MHz) the text names His22, Ala24, Gln29, 'Ala31', Met33 and Met35 as the residues with increased R1rho-derived R2 (Figure 6B: about 11, 15 and 12.5 s-1 at His22, Gln29 and Met35 against a baseline near 8-9 s-1), and the Tensor2 model-free fit returns exchange terms only in the metal-binding loop, at Ala24 (about 1.6 s-1), Gln29 (6.0), Gly31 (2.6), Ala32 (1.5), Met33 (3.3) and Met35 (4.3) in Figure S6C; His22 is fitted with S2 near 1.0 and no exchange term (SILB-J2, E-r1). Under the single-field Rex rule of 2026-10-20 this is exchange detected in the reference state without a population, so the call is 'possible'. The rest of the domain is rigid: S2 above 0.8 for most of the 63 fitted residues, tau_i about 70 ps, lower S2 only at the N terminus and in the loops between strands 3 and 4 and between 4 and 5, and no exchange term outside the loop (SILB-J1, J9). Adding one equivalent of Ag+ removes the excess transverse relaxation while leaving tumbling and fast motion unchanged, and the authors state that the apo protein does not simply exchange with a conformation close to the metal-bound state (SILB-J4, E-r2).

## Caveats

Call raised from unknown to possible on 2026-10-20 after the RelaxDB source paper (doi:10.1021/bi200005k) was read in full: exchange is detected in the reference state as single-field model-free Rex in the metal-binding loop, and under the single-field Rex rule decided that night (ledger/redraft_notes.json, _RULE_single_field_rex) author-reported Rex in the reference state counts as exchange detected without a population. Fixed caveat: single-field Rex can also come from anisotropic tumbling; a dispersion experiment would confirm. That caveat bites harder here than usual, because the preferred diffusion model is axially anisotropic; on the other hand R2 came from an R1rho decay chosen to suppress exchange, which makes the surviving loop terms harder to explain away. All Rex and S2 values are read from Supporting Information Figure S6, a plot; the text gives no table. The text prints 'Ala31' where the sequence has Gly31 and Ala32, so both positions are kept. Thr34 was not fitted and overlapping resonances were excluded (63 apo, 57 Ag+-bound residues). The abstract-only row SILB-r1 is superseded and no longer cited; the 2016 paper on the other construct remains an abstract. Both readers passed all four rows with no flags (ledger/sr_dual/S6_r6_17266_20261020221901_flags.json); the PDF adjudication is in ledger/adjudication_pdf_batch2.json.
