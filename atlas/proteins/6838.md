# Pseudomonas aeruginosa PSE-4 class A β-lactamase

RelaxDB entry `6838` · set RelaxDB-133 · Pseudomonas aeruginosa PSE-4 class A β-lactamase, wild-type mature enzyme (271 residues; the paper uses Ambler numbering)

## Answer

**Possible: exchange is detected in the free protein, but no population is measured.**

**Strict two-peak reading:** cannot tell. No regime is stated and no dispersion was recorded; S70 and A237 are broadened out and K73's weak peak points to slow or intermediate exchange, but no second peak set is reported.

The field dependence of 15N R2 at 50.6 and 60.8 MHz needs exchange terms at 32 amides, most prominently T128, L221, R234, S235 and G236 near the active site and R161 and R178 at the ends of the Ω loop. The catalytic S70 and A237 amides are broadened out. No CPMG or R1rho was recorded, so there is no rate or population.

## Caveats

Rex rests on two fields (the 81.0 MHz R2 was excluded) with quadratic field scaling. The BMRB 6838 deposition screen flags T128, L221, S235 and G236 and not R234, whose 500 MHz R2 is lowered by overlap with K192; the screen does not set the call. The full Rex and exchange tables (SI) were unavailable. No PDB entry is named for the S130 alternate, so no structure is matched.

## Open questions

- K73: highest-ranked model has no Rex and S2 0.94, against a weak amide peak and side-chain signals lost beyond C-gamma (J9, r2).
- Omega loop: Rex only at the hinges R161/R178 and none inside (J6, J10), fast H/D exchange at E171-L177 (J13) but protection at R161-N170 (J15); the authors reconcile these with a proposed cavity-filling motion that rests on TEM-1 MD (J17).
- Y105: two-timescale versus single-timescale model fit almost equally (J4, r1).
- Author label 'R161-E170' versus N170 in the sequence (r3); mapped here as R161-N170.
- Should the RelaxDB 6838 label at R234 (entry 212) be read against the 600 MHz data, given the 500 MHz R2 overlap with K192 that the authors report?
- Should the qualitative two-field Rex (no CPMG) count as us-ms evidence for the WT apo answer, or only as indirect support?
