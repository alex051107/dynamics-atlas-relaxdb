## Caveats

The abstract-level null is the closest thing to no_evidence, but without a stated window it stays unknown; the NMR collaborator is asked whether to obtain the 2012 full text. The BMRB 17701 deposition screen flags H60, I61, V63, V86, L90, Q121, Y123 and V138 by R2/R1, and its relaxation times appear to be in ms though labelled s; the screen does not set the call. The K117A/Q118A crystal 1Y7M shows higher LysM-loop B factors and a Cd2+/sulfate active-site artefact, not an alternate state (YKUD-J4, YKUD-J5, YKUD-r5). Searches did not use the alias LdtBs.

## Open questions

- No same-proposition conflicts. The free apoenzyme (2012 abstract: no preexisting exchange by dispersion) and the peptidoglycan-bound solid (2014: residual bulk motion) are different sample states.
- Numbering, not dynamics: the 2006 crystal paper and UniProt O34816 number residues as entry minus 3; the 2014 paper uses entry numbering.
- Should the 2012 Structure full text and SI be obtained? It is the likely source of the BMRB 17701 relaxation set and of the dispersion null, and without it YKUD-J1 stays unresolved.
- Bulk REDOR S and bulk R1rho of the peptidoglycan-bound protein span ps to tens of microseconds; keep them in the undetermined window, or split them between ps-ns and us-ms?
