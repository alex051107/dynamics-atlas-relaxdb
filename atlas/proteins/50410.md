# Anabaena (Nostoc) sp. PCC 7120 All4940 (AnaCTDH)

RelaxDB entry `50410` · set RelaxDB-133 · Anabaena (Nostoc) sp. PCC 7120 All4940 (AnaCTDH), a C-terminal-domain homolog of the orange carotenoid protein, with C-terminal His6 tag

## Answer

**Possible: exchange is detected in the free protein, but no population is measured.**

**Strict two-peak reading:** cannot tell. No regime is stated; the β5–β6 loop amides S102–G105 are broadened beyond detection and no second peak set is reported.

Single-field 15N relaxation with a fully anisotropic model-free fit puts the largest Rex, together with signals broadened beyond detection, in the β5–β6 loop T101–G105 next to the putative dimer interface. No dispersion, CEST or R1rho was recorded, so there is no rate, population or minor state. Crystal and MD evidence show a C-terminal tail that takes several positions, also without a rate.

Fallback statement: May have an alternate conformation of the β5–β6 loop (T101–G105) near the putative dimer interface and of the C-terminal tail, based on 15N relaxation and exchange broadening of the apo protein plus crystal structures and MD (doi:10.1007/s12104-020-09976-1; doi:10.1016/j.bbabio.2020.148214).

## Matching structures

What the papers relate to the states:

- **6S5L**: Apo crystal (pH 5, phosphate, no urea) whose 12 chains split six and six between two C-terminal tail orientations, one more perpendicular to the body and one nearer the closed 6FEJ tail. Chain counts, not populations, and no rate; this concerns the tail, not the β5–β6 loop exchange.
- **6FEJ**: Apo crystal grown with 1 M urea after 2 M urea pretreatment, Cys103 disulfide-linked dimer, with the C-terminal tail turned inward to cap the carotenoid cavity. Both A-type and F-type dimer interfaces coexist in the lattice.
- **5UI2**: Homolog holo-OCP from Arthrospira maxima; its C-terminal domain is the open, outward-tail reference. Different protein and ligand state.

## Other conditions (not the reference state)

- Apo MD from 6FEJ, six 2 µs runs: the tail opens in 2 of 6 runs through a perpendicular intermediate; run counts are not populations (ALL4940-J8, ALL4940-50410_20261017115440-r6).
- MD of a docked holo model opens in 2 of 6 runs (ALL4940-J19, ALL4940-50410_20261017115440-r7); L128D stalls at the intermediate (ALL4940-J20, ALL4940-50410_20261017115440-r8); L137D flips in all three runs (ALL4940-J21, ALL4940-50410_20261017115440-r9). Simulation only.
- Wild-type holo (carotenoid-bound) All4940: unknown. No experimental structure or dynamics measurement (ALL4940-J17).
- Oxidized disulfide-linked apo dimer (6FEJ crystal): both dimer types coexist and the authors write that transitions 'may occur', with no solution population or rate (ALL4940-J5, ALL4940-r4).

## Caveats

The BMRB 50410 deposition screen cannot test the β5–β6 loop: S102–G105 have no deposited data and T101 is not flagged, while its R2/R1 flags (E70, G81–A83, A99, H122, I125, K126) are sites the text does not name; the screen does not set the call. τc of 10.0 ± 0.3 ns at 35 °C (ALL4940-J2) for a 16 kDa protein raises the question of self-association, which the NMR collaborator is asked about. The literature search used only the name 'All4940 protein', so missing dispersion data are a search gap.

## Open questions

- Source numbering, not a dynamics conflict: the 2018 paper uses native numbering (C103, W102, Y26; entry = native + 1), the 2021 NMR paper uses 6FEJ numbering equal to the entry (C104, W103, Y27), and the 2020 BBA paper uses entry numbering for residues (L82, L128, L137, W110) but the native label C103F for the entry-C104 mutant; Gln132 in the 2018 paper still maps to entry E133 and stays unmapped, and Asn94 in a 2020 caption conflicts with Q94.
- Supplementary Fig. 8F caption names HCP4 while the panel label and main text name HCP1 (r20); HCP1 was used.
- Membrane-uptake endpoint for C103F: 100% after 1 h in 2018 (r13) versus 80.30% in 2020 (21-r17) under the same nominal assay (33 °C, 12 µM dimers, 48 µM canthaxanthin); binding endpoint, not a dynamics conflict.
- The BMRB-50410 sample is apo and reduced and the authors treat it as monomeric, yet tau_c is 10.0 ns at 35 °C for a 16 kDa protein; should the entry be carried as apo monomer, or as an apo monomer/dimer mixture whose R2 (and the R2/R1 flags) may include self-association?
- Should the oxidized disulfide-linked apo dimer (crystals) and the reduced apo monomer (NMR) be carried as separate sample states for the wild-type apo answer?
