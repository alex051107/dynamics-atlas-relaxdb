## Answer

**Unknown: not measured, or the key paper is not readable in full.**

**Strict two-peak reading:** cannot tell. No row states an exchange regime for the entry construct; the paper reports a single resonance set (no tetramer doubling) and amides missing at 37 °C without a mechanism.

No dispersion, R1rho or CEST exists for APOBEC2 (R18306-J2, search_insufficient). The 700 MHz relaxation data are readable only as an overall tumbling time of 17 ns (R18306-J1), and the amides missing from the truncated construct at 37 °C may reflect solvent exchange, precipitation or exchange (R18306-J3). Loop variability appears only in models and crystals (R18306-J5, R18306-J6).

## Matching structures

What the papers relate to the states:

- **2NYT**: Truncated human A2 crystal tetramer in which the alpha1-beta1 loop is a loop in tetramer-interface subunits and a short strand in dimer-interface subunits; the authors attribute the difference to packing. Crystal snapshots, no solution population or rate, and the loop is disordered in their solution model.

## Other conditions (not the reference state)

- Full-length A2 (1-224), 37 °C: the N-terminal tail M1-E40, absent from the entry, is highly mobile by linewidths and shifts (R18306-J4).
- Self-association: the entry construct is a monomer by SEC-MALS, tumbling and RDCs at 25 °C, unlike the crystal tetramer (R18306-J7).
- Mouse A2 2RPZ homolog: different loop placement, not a measured transition (R18306-J6).
