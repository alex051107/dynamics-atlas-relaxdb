# Human CCL5/RANTES variant 5P12-RANTES-E66S (the entry sequence; engineered N terminus Q0-S9 and E66S

RelaxDB entry `19335` · set RelaxDB-133 · Human CCL5/RANTES variant 5P12-RANTES-E66S (the entry sequence; engineered N terminus Q0-S9 and E66S, author Q0-S68 = entry 1-69)

## Answer

**Possible: exchange is detected in the free protein, but no population is measured.**

**Strict two-peak reading:** cannot tell. For the entry construct the source reports one concentration-independent resonance set and states no exchange regime for the raised R2 at M5-S9 or the broadening at Y14 and G32; the separate monomer and dimer peak sets reported for native-N-terminus E66S CCL5 belong to self-association, not to an alternate monomer state.

In free 5P12-RANTES-E66S the µs-ms signs are qualitative: raised 15N R2 at M5–S9 inside the frayed N-terminal tail, and exchange broadening at Y14 and G32 that the authors exclude from core averages. No dispersion was recorded, so there is no rate, population or Δω, and the construct stays monomeric. The call is possible on exchange broadening alone; the two-peak observations in CCL5 (monomer and dimer, 12AAA14 heterogeneity) are self-association or anion effects in other constructs and are kept out.

## Other conditions (not the reference state)

- Native-N-terminus E66S CCL5, pH 4.8 acetate, 310 K and 293 K: separate monomer and dimer peaks (IM/ID 0.45 to 0.13 on cooling); self-association, not an alternate monomer state (R19335-J10, R19335-r8, R19335-r9).
- Native-N-terminus E66S CCL5, pH 4.5, 37 °C, 1 versus 20 µM: two signal sets at 20 µM from monomer and dimer; self-association (R19335-J11, R19335-r34).
- CCL5-12AAA14 mutant, pH 3.2, 298 K: heterogeneous spectrum that sulfate collapses to one dimer set, and missing basic-residue peaks possibly from intermediate exchange with sulfate; unknown (R19335-J15, R19335-J16).
- Fos-Choline-12 micelles, pH 3.8: core NMR-dark at 25 °C, back at 60 °C as a non-native helical ensemble; artefact suspected (R19335-J7).
- Receptor peptide or Evasin-4 bound: fast or intermediate binding exchange; binding kinetics, not an apo state (R19335-J18 to R19335-J22).

## Caveats

The entry construct is itself an engineered variant; native CCL5 data are other constructs. The deposited core averages equal the paper's 37 °C values although the BMRB metadata says 273 K. All entry data are in phosphate without a control. The deposition readback (R2/R1 screen flags Y15) is a lead only.

## Open questions

- Temperature of the deposited set: the paper's Figure 4 and Methods give 37 °C, BMRB 19335 metadata gives 273 K; the deposited core averages equal the paper's 37 °C values (r1, deposition readback).
- Detergent buffer for the FC-12 samples: Figure 6 says 50 mM KH2PO4, Methods say 50 mM DCOONa, and FC-12 is 1% in Figure 6 but 0.5% in the text (r3, r4).
- Is the RelaxDB 19335 set the 2013 37 °C 5P12-RANTES-E66S data, so the 273 K in the BMRB metadata is a deposition error?
- Should native-N-terminus CCL5 data (E66S, P9S, 12AAA14, K25S) count toward this 5P12 entry only as inference, as done here, given that their N termini and oligomer states differ?
