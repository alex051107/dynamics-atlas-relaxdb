# BLAC_CPMG: deposited structures between open 2GDN:A and closed 7A74:A

Ambler numbering (the numbering of 2GDN chain A and of the NMR papers; BLAC-J1/J2 numbering_note). Mobile = the active-site segments carrying the apo-CPMG group-fit residues F68, C69, T71, E166, E168, N170, T215, A218 (BLAC-J1) and the broadened S70, S130 (BLAC-J2), plus the segments that show two conformations in the G132N crystal 7A74 (Ser104-Ile105, Ser130, loop 214-216; BLAC-r9 / BLAC-J17): 68-72 (S70 strand-helix junction), 101-107 (S104-I105 loop), 128-133 (SDN loop), 164-180 (Omega loop; 2GDN helix 167-171), 213-221 (214-216 loop and T215/A218). The broadened T235/T237 lie on the core beta3 strand 230-238 and stay in the core. Core = the rest, excluding 144-146 (Ambler insertion 145A-D cannot be represented by an integer auth_seq_id). 'open' = resting wild type 2GDN chain A (BLAC-r15: 'wild-type BlaC modelled from the crystal structure 2GDN'); 'closed' = G132N 7A74 chain A, the only deposited structure whose authors tie its double conformations to a second NMR state (BLAC-r9: 'matching the two states seen by NMR'; it is a mutant, grown with 100 mM phosphate). The authors describe the 7A74 differences as a cis peptide, side chains and the 214-216 loop, so a Calpha comparison is expected to be weakly informative; the inhibitor adducts 3CG5 (clavulanate) and 4DF6 (avibactam) of BLAC-r3 are placed in the table for comparison. PO4 is counted as a ligand because the anion site is the point at issue (BLAC-J2; 5NJ2 is deposited as 'bound to phosphate').
